**Rideable Sulfur Cubes** is an official companion mod from **Aevora Labs** that allows players to tame, pet, and ride **Sulfur Cubes** while preserving their native hopping physics and bouncy movement archetype.

Available natively for **Minecraft 26.3+** on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**.

---

## ⚡ Features

* 🧪 **Slime Taming:** Feed baby sulfur cubes slime balls to grow them, or feed adult cubes to tame them into loyal mounts.
* 🐾 **Petting & Bonding:** Right-click an owned sulfur cube with an empty hand to pet them and mount up.
* 🧭 **Rider Steering:** Full WASD input translation into the cube's internal angular hopping physics.
* 🛡 **Fall Damage Negation:** Ride fearlessly off cliffs; riders take zero fall damage when landing on their bouncy cube mount.
* 🏆 **Slimy Slingshot:** Includes a custom in-game advancement earned by petting and riding your first tamed cube.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Find a Sulfur Cube** in the world.
2. Hold a **Slime Ball** and right-click:
   - If baby: grows into an adult.
   - If adult: tames the cube (emits heart particles).
3. With an **empty hand**, right-click your tamed cube to mount it.
4. Use standard **WASD** keys to steer your hops!

---

## 📦 Architecture & Compatibility

* **Minecraft Version:** `26.3` or newer (`>=26.3`)
* **Loaders Supported:**
  * **Fabric** (Fabric Loader `>=0.19.5`)
  * **NeoForge** (NeoForge `>=26.3.0.6-beta`)
* **Java Version:** Java 25+
* **Non-destructive:** Built with clean Mixins targeting vanilla `SulfurCube` without overriding registries or breaking other mods.
